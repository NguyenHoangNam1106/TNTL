## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an inspiring, forward-looking academic portal for Trường THPT Trần Cao Vân. Embracing the vision of "Nơi Ươm Mầm Ước Mơ & Khát Vọng" (Nurturing Dreams and Aspirations), the interface departs from conventional, bureaucratic institutional portals in favor of a cosmic, futuristic, and aspirational aesthetic. It treats education as an exploratory universe where each student's potential is a luminous star.

### Aesthetic Direction: Neo-Cosmic Glassmorphism
The aesthetic combines deep astronomical nightscapes with crisp, crystalline glass surfaces and vivid chromatic nebulas. By merging strict institutional clarity with the awe of space exploration, the interface conveys prestige, energy, and relentless curiosity.

- **Atmosphere:** Deep void tones contrasted against radiant auroral sweeps (electric cyan, stellar violet, celestial pink).
- **Surface Quality:** Translucent frosted panes, subtle perimeter light glints, and luminous focal glows that simulate depth in zero gravity.
- **Tone of Voice:** Inspiring, empowering, precise, and youthful without sacrificing administrative rigor.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an ultra-deep cosmic spectrum, punctured by vibrant chromatic accents representing knowledge, discovery, and aspiration.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (Sky / Electric Cyan - `#38BDF8`):** The beacon color. Used for interactive states, primary calls-to-action, key badges, and focused active indicators. Supporting tints include Sky Highlight (`#7DD3FC`) and Deep Azure (`#0284C7`).
- **Secondary (Nebula Violet - `#818CF8`):** Represents academic wisdom and depth. Paired with Astral Purple (`#C084FC`) for gradient transitions and secondary navigational elements.
- **Tertiary (Supernova Pink - `#F472B6`):** Reserved for energetic highlights, achievements, honors, and student milestone callouts.
- **Neutral (Cosmic Night - `#070B19`):** The infinite canvas backdrop. Step layers shift into Midnight Indigo (`#0B112C`) and Deep Slate (`#0F172A`).

### Atmospheric & Surface Colors
- **Glass Base:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` layered over gradient-bearing canvas areas.
- **Glass Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` providing structured light refraction.
- **Foreground Text:** Crystalline White (`#F8FAFC`) for primary headers and data; Celestial Mist (`#CBD5E1`) for readable body; Starlight Cyan (`#38BDF8`) for thematic link highlights.

## Typography

Typography establishes an institutional standard paired with contemporary vitality.

- **Headlines & Titles (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly curvature, balanced geometry, and modern optimism. Tight letter-spacing gives major titles an authoritative yet energetic presence.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and systematically engineered to handle dense academic data, class schedules, grade books, and official announcements without visual fatigue.
- **Code & Identifiers (JetBrains Mono):** Deployed selectively for student IDs, classroom codes, and system parameters to reinforce the digital campus motif.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal uses a fluid 12-column grid system bounded inside a maximum content width of 1440px to ensure crisp rendering on widescreen monitors while preventing interface detachment.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (1.5rem) gutters, 40px (2.5rem) exterior margins. Academic dashboard layouts use asymmetric 8+4 column configurations for news/announcement feeds and side schedules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 24px (1.5rem) exterior margins. Secondary informational panels reflow beneath primary content feeds.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 16px (1rem) exterior margins. All interactive blocks stack into a focused vertical flow with persistent floating navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque cast drop shadows; instead, it utilizes physical optical depth, translucent glass tiers, and localized ambient halos.

- **Canvas Ground (Base):** Deep `#070B19` with ambient radial color bleeds (e.g., cyan and violet soft glows situated at 10% opacity in canvas corners and major section breaks).
- **Glass Layer 1 (Cards, Modules, Feeds):** Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surrounded by a subtle hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Glass Layer 2 (Hover States, Active Cards, Popovers):** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, illuminated by an upper border specular gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25), rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05))`.
- **Cosmic Elevation (Overlays, Modals, Critical Flyouts):** Background `rgba(11, 17, 44, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, framed with `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` and an ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a structured `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius) philosophy. This creates rounded, approachable corners that keep elements feeling sleek, geometric, and precise without deteriorating into toy-like or cartoonish visuals.

- **Standard Controls & Badges (0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, chips, text inputs, schedule tiles.
- **Panels & Display Cards (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Academic feed modules, portal utility cards, hero containers.
- **Large Modals & Feature Displays (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Major announcement banners and spotlight interactive modules.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Notification counters and categorical status indicators use 9999px for rapid status scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid or gradient fill transitioning from `#0284C7` to `#38BDF8`. Crisp white text (`#F8FAFC`), font weight 600. Includes an outer glow on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Glass):** Frosted panel styling with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` fill, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` border, and `#F8FAFC` label. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with a subtle cyan border glow.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#7DD3FC` text, gaining an underline and soft glow during active states.

### Cards
- Constructed with Glass Layer 1 specifications.
- Header sections feature a crisp 1px divider border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Interactive cards feature an edge-glow transition on hover: the upper-left perimeter shifts toward primary cyan, paired with a -4px vertical lift.

### Chips & Badges
- **Academic Tags (Department, Grade Level):** Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, text `#7DD3FC`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **Achievement & Honors:** Background `rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.12)`, text `#F472B6`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `rgba(11, 17, 44, 0.65)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state applies `border-color: #38BDF8` and an ethereal cyan inner ring glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Semi-translucent dark base. When checked, filled with vibrant `#38BDF8` featuring an illuminated starlight-white check icon.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Student Milestone Tracker:** A linear progress bar rendered in a radiant gradient from `#818CF8` through `#38BDF8`, punctuated by glowing starlight nodes.
- **Portal Schedule Matrix:** Time slots constructed with modular glass tiles; active ongoing sessions feature a pulsating cyan left-border accent and faint violet illumination.
